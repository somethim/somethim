The platform

Zennit builds SaaS products, open-source packages, and client software. My core work is the shared backend that everything runs on: a nine-module Laravel platform covering identity, authorization, billing, notifications, files, auditing, and delivery — the infrastructure each new product inherits instead of rebuilding.

Identity & access

The identity module is the deepest piece: ten external OAuth providers behind one account model, passwordless email login, account linking across providers, TOTP multi-factor, and session management with auditable termination — every revocation traceable to who ended which session, when, and why.

  • Authorization is policy-based and shared across products, with an open-sourced attribute-based access control package (zennit-dev/abac) extracted from it.
  • Delivery is repeatable by design: Docker images, Ansible playbooks, and GitHub/GitLab CI pipelines shared across all six products.
