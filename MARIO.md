*Product Design*

Designed the full product: competitive analysis, branding, user personas, user flows, wireframes, app UI, and marketing website.

### addCar — Brand Identity
*Brand Identity*

Albanian car rental company built on a single brand promise: the smart way. Had no visual identity before this project. The work covered brand strategy, logo system, interior design direction for three branch concepts, fleet vehicles, signage, uniforms, and marketing materials. The entire identity is engineered from one geometric detail: the 134.65° apex of the letter "D", which becomes a standalone symbol, a repeating pattern, and a spatial language across every touchpoint.
