About avatid
avatid is an intelligent appointment management platform that uses personalized AI avatars to help users 
manage their beauty and health appointments. The mobile application (iOS/Android) creates a visual 
identity for each user: an avatar that remembers past treatments, tracks appointment history, and 
proactively reminds users when it's time for their next haircut, dentist visit, physiotherapy session, or self-
care appointment.

Conclusion
Market Context(
The beauty and health appointment space is fragmented across multiple platforms. Users struggle to 
track when they last visited different providers and often miss routine appointments. No unified 
system exists that learns from behavior to suggest when the next appointment should be.
User Behavior(
68% of users miss routine appointments not because they don't value them, but because they forget 
or don't realize how much time has passed. The core friction isn't cost, it's the fear of forgetting or 
missing scheduled appointments.
Privacy Concerns(
Users possess valuable appointment data but fear how AI systems will use their 
information. They prioritize control and transparency over automated convenience.

Strength: Good for service providers
Weakness: Provider-centric, not client-centric
Insight: Focuses on helping providers, not 
users
Google Calendar
